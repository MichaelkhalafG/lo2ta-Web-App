# Market values for the calculator: research register (2026-11-24)

Phase 6 step 1 (PLAN 4.3, plan approved by Michael 2026-11-24). Internal: sources stay here, in the dashboard next
to each value and in the findings table for the client; the public site shows only the range and "checked <month
year>" (J10). Nothing here is loaded into settings until Michael has reviewed the findings table.

Method: public pages only, read once each at a slow pace, robots rules respected (a page that refuses automated
reading is not read and its figures are not counted). Sources counted as independent only when they do not come from
the same origin (three press articles on one study count as one). Excluded by rule: aqarexit and other resale
platforms (competitors), listing and broker marketing pages, forum and social posts, contract templates on lawyer
sites, religious-ruling sites quoting a contract. All pages read on 2026-11-24 (Cairo).

## Findings, value by value

### V1 Cancellation deduction (`penalty_min_bp`, `penalty_max_bp`, `penalty_basis`)

- Supported by at least two independent sources: **10% of the total price** (S1 lowest generation, S4, S5).
- Above 10%: one independent source only (S1: 15%, 20%, 30% in newer contracts). By J1 the upper end needs a
  second source or the client's decision.
- Basis: every readable source takes it from the **total price** (S1 says so explicitly; S4, S5). No readable source
  for the amount paid.
- Now in settings (provisional, aqarexit's): 10%–15% of the total price.
- Context for the client's lawyer: a court-ordered cancellation for the developer's breach means a full refund with no
  deduction (S10, S11; one origin); having paid more than 75% blocks an automatic cancellation (same origin).

### V2 Refund after cancellation (`refund_rule`, `refund_years_fixed`)

- One readable source (S6): the rest is returned "through installments spread over a lengthy period", period not
  given. No readable source for "the same period as the payments" or for a fixed number of years.
- Now in settings (provisional): the same period as the payments (`mirror`). By J4 this is the client's setting.

### V3 Late-payment penalty (`late_fee_bp`)

- No readable private-market source with a figure. Government social housing: 1.5–2.5% a month (S15, reference
  only). Al Arabiya (not counted): 1–2% of the installment.
- Now in settings: 0 (the line is off). The formula takes one percentage of the overdue amount, once; a monthly rate
  would need a period setting.

### V4 Developer transfer fee (`transfer_fee_mode`, `transfer_fee_bp`, `transfer_fee_fixed`)

- S2 (study): 1–5% of the unit value. S8 (developers' association): formerly 3–5%, now dropped as unlawful; the
  government charge about 1%; some developers a flat EGP 30,000–40,000. S7 (lawyer): usually 5–10%, up to 15%. S9
  (parliament): up to 15% (S8 denies it).
- Supported by at least two independent sources: **1%–5% of the unit value** (S2 and S8). 5% appears in S2, S7, S8.
  Up to 15%: S7 and S9, disputed by S8.
- Now in settings (provisional): a percentage, 0. The calculator needs one default; the seller's own figure in the
  wizard (step 3) already replaces it for a listed unit.

### V5 Conditions on a transfer (information only)

- No readable private-market source for a minimum paid share, blocked periods or approval time. Government units need
  written approval and dues paid to date. Stays a client text (or is left out).

### V6 Maintenance deposit (information only)

- Supported by two independent sources: **8%–10% of the unit price** (S3 via the study: up to 10%; S12: 8–10%).
- Whether it moves to the buyer on a transfer: no source.

### V7 Discount rate (`discount_rate_bp`, used only if the present-value line is switched on)

- Central Bank of Egypt overnight deposit rate **20.00% from the MPC of 2026-10-29** (S13; S14 headline). The line
  stays off (decided 2026-11-17); J7 picks this rate if it is ever switched on.

### Not researched

- The 50% deep-loss line is a display threshold, not a market value (J6).
- Watch before launch: a draft law on the real estate market is reported (a withdrawal period, a reserve for refunds,
  possibly rules on transfer fees); not passed as of 2026-11-24.
